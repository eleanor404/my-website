Tips for visiting the Lake District

By caring for our National Park, we can continue to make it a special place for all to visit. Follow our tips, tools and frequently asked questions which have everything you need to know for a smooth and enjoyable visit.

Sustainable travel

Let’s keep the Lake District special – discover alternative ways to get here and explore its stunning surroundings. Whether by boat, bus, bike, or on foot, traveling sustainably allows you to experience the beauty of the Lake District in a more immersive and eco-friendly way. Help us look after this spectacular landscape by leaving the car at home, helping to reduce traffic and parking pressures, meaning you can enjoy more views and some stress-free, sustainable travel. The Lake District is in the North West of England, with Manchester to the South and Carlise to the North. From sitting back and watching the landscape go by on a train, to connecting with the National Park by boat, let the journey be part of your adventure!
